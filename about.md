# Roll with It: A Casino Night in Your Pocket
Walk onto the floor, pull up a chair, and see how far $1,000 on the house can take you. Whether you are riding a hot streak at the craps table, putting it all on 17, or doubling down against a dealer with opinions, Roll with It brings the noise and nerve of a casino night straight to your device.

No real money, no real regrets. And when your luck runs out, Uncle Vinny covers you. For a price.

## FEATURES TO FUEL YOUR NIGHT OUT:

* Three Tables, One Bankroll: Move between craps, roulette, and blackjack whenever you like. Your chips and your reputation follow you across the floor.
* Real Craps Action: Play the Pass Line, Don't Pass, Come, Don't Come, Field, place numbers, hardways, and odds on a full betting layout.
* A Spin of the Wheel: Bet on red or black, odd or even, dozens, and columns, or press and hold any number for splits, streets, and corners.
* Blackjack by the Book: Hit, stand, double, and split your way to 21. Blackjack pays 3 to 2, and the dealer stands on all 17s.
* Uncle Vinny's Family Loans: Cleaned out? Vinny will spot you at 10% interest. Then 25%. Then 50%. Then 100%, plus a favor. After four loans, you're on your own.
* Dress the Part: Design your own high roller, from skin tone and hair to outfits, shades, and jewelry or a beard, then give yourself a table name.
* Keep Score: The Ledger tracks your stats at every table, and the Trophy Case holds 18 trophies to unlock.
* Learn as You Play: New to the tables? Teaching hints appear under the dealer, and How to Play walks you through each game. Turn the hints and the dealer banter off whenever you like.
* Play on iPhone and iPad: The game runs full screen on both, in portrait.
* The Whole Game for Everyone: Nothing is locked. If you enjoy the tables, you can leave an optional tip for the dealer as a thank-you.

## HOW TO PLAY:

* Tap Pull Up a Chair, then design your character and name yourself.
* Hit the Floor and pick a table.
* Tap a chip to choose your bet size, then tap the felt to place your bet.
* Smash ROLL, SPIN, or DEAL, and let the night decide your fate!
* Running low? Call Vinny. Just remember that he always collects.
* Your next big night is just a roll away. Download Roll with It today and let the good times roll!

*Roll with It is for entertainment only. All chips, winnings, and loans are play money with no real-world value, and they cannot be cashed out. The game does not offer real-money gambling.*
