# Privacy Policy for Roll with It

**Last Updated: November 2, 2026**

Your privacy is incredibly important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Roll with It** handles information when you use our iOS and iPadOS application.

The short answer is: **We don't ask who you are, and your game data stays on your device. If you leave "Anonymous usage stats" on, the app sends anonymous stats about how the game is played. You can turn them off at any time.**

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
* **No Personal Data:** We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, or physical location.
* **No Account Creation:** You do not need to create an account or log in to use any features of the app.
* **No Advertising or Cross-App Tracking:** The app has no ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
* **Anonymous Usage Stats:** The app collects the anonymous usage information described in Section 3, unless you turn it off.

## 2. On-Device Storage
Your game data is stored **locally on your own device**. This includes your table name, your character's look, your bankroll, stats, trophies, loans from Uncle Vinny, and your settings.
* We do not have access to this data, and the app does not send it to us, except for the anonymous usage stats described in Section 3.
* The name you enter for your character is never included in those stats.
* Your device's own backups, such as iCloud Backup, may include this data, depending on your settings.
* If you delete the app, this locally stored data will be removed from your device.

## 3. Anonymous Usage Stats
To help improve the game, the app uses [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), a privacy-focused analytics service. This setting is **on by default**. To turn it off, open **House Rules** and switch off **Anonymous usage stats**. The app then stops sending these stats.

When the setting is on, the app may send:
* **Gameplay:** Screens and tables viewed, the kinds of bets used, game results and stats in play money, play-money loans, trophies unlocked, and settings changed.
* **Character choices:** Your in-game character's type and appearance, such as skin tone, hair, outfit, and accessories. These describe the character you design, not you.
* **Tips:** The size and price of any tip you give. This does not include your payment details.
* **Device and app details:** Device model, operating system version, app version, screen size, language, region, time zone, and display preferences.
* **App usage:** Details such as the number of sessions, how long sessions last, and how many days you have used the app.
* **An anonymized identifier:** A scrambled (hashed) ID that lets events from the same installation be counted together. It does not contain your name or contact details.

According to TelemetryDeck, the identifier is hashed before it is stored, the analytics data cannot be traced back to you, and IP addresses from the app are not stored on its servers. You can read more in [TelemetryDeck's privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

Because these stats are not tied to your identity, we generally cannot find or delete the stats from a particular person.

## 4. Tips and Purchases
Roll with It is free to play, and the optional "Tip the dealer" in-app purchases use real money. Tips do not unlock anything or add chips to your bankroll. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store, and we never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

All chips, winnings, and loans in the game are play money. They have no real-world value and cannot be cashed out.

## 5. Third-Party Services
Besides the services described above, Roll with It does not include third-party frameworks or SDKs that collect data, outside of standard Apple App Store services (such as anonymous crash reports provided directly by Apple if you have opted in to share them with developers).

The House Rules screen includes a link to our X.com page. It opens outside the app, and X's own privacy policy applies there.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Roll with It is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future app updates or changes in legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
