# Roll with It — Support & Feedback

Thank you for playing Roll with It! We want to ensure you have the best possible experience at the craps, roulette, and blackjack tables.

If you are encountering an issue, have a feature request, or need assistance, please use one of the methods below to get in touch.

## How to Get Help

### 1. Report an Issue or Feature Request
The fastest way to get support is to open an issue directly on our project tracker. This allows you to see if other users are experiencing the same issue and track the progress of a fix.

*   [**Open a Support Ticket / Bug Report**](https://github.com/AnnawanDev/Roll-with-it-support/issues)

*Note: You will need a free GitHub account to open an issue here.*

### 2. Contact Us on X
You can also reach us on X at [@RollWithItApp](https://x.com/RollWithItApp).

*Note: GitHub issues and posts on X are public, so please don't include personal information.*

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is this real gambling? Can I win real money?**  
**A:** No. All chips, winnings, and loans in the game are play money. They have no real-world value and cannot be cashed out.

**Q: I ran out of money. What now?**  
**A:** Uncle Vinny will lend you more, at an interest rate that goes up with each loan. After four loans he cuts you off, and you can start a new night with a fresh $1,000.

**Q: How do I start over?**  
**A:** Open the menu at a table, choose **House Rules**, then choose **Start a new night**. You get a fresh $1,000 and Vinny forgets your loans, and your trophies stay.

**Q: What do tips do?**  
**A:** "Tip the dealer" is an optional thank-you that uses real money. Tips don't unlock anything or add chips to your bankroll, and every player gets the whole game.

**Q: How do I get a refund for a tip?**  
**A:** Purchases are handled by Apple, so refund requests go through Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Q: How do I turn off the anonymous usage stats?**  
**A:** Open **House Rules** and switch off **Anonymous usage stats**. You can read more in our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Q: Which devices does the game run on?**  
**A:** iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The game plays in portrait.
